Delete your Muhit account

Muhit works without an account. If you created one to sync your words, you can delete it, and everything synced with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Muhit and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email admin@muhit.site from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Muhit account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel billing separately in your store account: Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions.

What is deleted

Your account, email address and sign-in details, and your synced folders, saved words, favourites, practice progress, in-app reports and account-linked Mi'rāb request records. Copies in our provider's backups are overwritten within a short period.

What is kept

Mi'rāb free-analysis counters, keyed by your account ID and hashed email/device identifiers, remain after account deletion to prevent trial resets and expire 12 months after last use. Cached analyses are not linked to an account and expire after 90 days. If you used Sign in with Apple, a token needed to revoke access may remain in a restricted retry queue for up to 30 days. Words stored only on your phone stay there until you delete them or the app. Store billing and RevenueCat customer records are separate from Muhit account deletion; contact admin@muhit.site for help with a data-deletion request involving those providers.
