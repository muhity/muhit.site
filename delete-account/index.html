Delete your Muhit account

Muhit works without an account. If you created one to sync your words, you can delete it, and everything synced with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Muhit and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email admin@muhit.site from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

Your account, email address and sign-in details, and your synced folders, saved words, favourites, practice progress and Mi'rāb usage counts. Copies in our provider's backups are overwritten within a short period.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Mi'rāb analyses are stored without any link to who asked and expire after 90 days. If you used Sign in with Apple, a token needed to tell Apple to revoke access is kept until that succeeds, at most 30 days. Words stored only on your phone stay there until you delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.
