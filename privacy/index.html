Privacy Policy

Who we are

Muhit ("we") is a business registered in Sweden and is the controller responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at admin@muhit.site. This policy covers the Muhit app and the website muhit.site.

The short version

Muhit works without an account, and then everything stays on your device. If you create an account, we store your email address and the learning data you choose to sync. Mi'rāb sends the sentences you submit to an AI provider to analyse them; we keep a copy of each analysis, with no link to who asked, for up to 90 days so repeated sentences can be answered instantly. Muhit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Data that stays on your device

The dictionary, your search history and recent lookups, saved words, folders, favourites, practice history, settings and study reminders are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Reminders are scheduled by your phone; we do not send them. Deleting the app removes this data, unless it was synced to an account.

If you create an account

We store: your email address; a securely hashed password, or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, an identifier and the email address that service shares with us (Apple may give a private relay address); your folders, saved words, favourites and practice progress so they sync between your devices; and technical records needed to run the service, such as sign-in times and the date your data last changed. Search history, settings and reminders are not synced.

Mi'rāb

When you analyse a sentence, it is sent from our server to OpenAI, which produces the analysis and returns it to your device. We ask OpenAI not to store these requests; under its API policy it may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse and then deletes them, and it does not use them to train its models. So that a sentence someone has already asked about can be answered instantly, we store the sentence and its analysis for up to 90 days, with no account, name or other link to who sent it. We do not record which account analysed which sentence. To keep usage fair, we count how many analyses each account makes per day.

Emails

We send account emails, such as confirming your address and resetting your password, through Resend. If you write to admin@muhit.site, we use your message only to reply and keep it as long as needed to handle your request. We do not send marketing emails.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

We process account, sync and Mi'rāb data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We keep usage counts and security records to protect the service from abuse (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep records where the law requires it (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Who processes data for us

Supabase (account database and sign-in, hosted in the EU, Ireland); OpenAI (Mi'rāb analysis, United States); Resend (sending account emails, EU region); Hostinger (our admin@muhit.site mailbox); GitHub Pages (hosting muhit.site, which records visitors' IP addresses for security); and Apple or Google, if you use their sign-in or buy a subscription through their stores. They may only use the data to provide their service to us. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, such as to the United States, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, synced folders, saved words, favourites, practice progress and usage counts straight away; copies in our provider's backups are overwritten within a short period. An Apple authorization token awaiting revocation is kept in a restricted retry queue until revocation succeeds, with a 30-day expiry checked by our scheduled worker. Stored Mi'rāb analyses, which are not linked to any account, are deleted after 90 days. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw any consent. Email admin@muhit.site and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY, imy.se) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Accounts and Mi'rāb are for people aged 13 or older. The offline dictionary does not collect personal data, so it can be used at any age. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Muhit's data handling changes, and show the date of the latest version at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
