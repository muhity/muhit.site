Privacy Policy

Who we are

Muhit ("we") is a business registered in Sweden and is the controller responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at admin@muhit.site. This policy covers the Muhit app and the website muhit.site.

The short version

You can use the offline dictionary and study tools without an account. Their learning data stays on your device unless you choose account sync. In versions with purchases enabled, RevenueCat also processes subscription identifiers and purchase status, including an anonymous identifier when you are signed out. If you create an account, we store your email address and the learning data you choose to sync. Mi'rab AI sends the sentences you submit to an AI provider to analyse them. To limit each person’s free analyses, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your email address and of a random ID the app creates for your device. We keep a copy of each analysis, with no link to who asked, for up to 90 days so repeated sentences can be answered instantly. Muhit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, we never sell or share your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Data that stays on your device

The dictionary, search history and recent lookups, settings and study reminders stay on your device. Saved words, folders, favourites and practice progress are also stored on your device and are uploaded if you choose account sync. Reminders are scheduled by your phone; we do not send them. Deleting the app removes this data, unless it was synced to an account.

If you create an account

We store: your email address; a securely hashed password, or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, an identifier and the email address that service shares with us (Apple may give a private relay address); your folders, saved words, favourites and practice progress so they sync between your devices; a record of your sign-up agreement (the version of the terms and privacy policy you accepted, and when); and technical records needed to run the service, such as sign-in times and the date your data last changed. Search history, settings and reminders are not synced.

Mi'rab AI

When you analyse a sentence, it is sent from our server to OpenAI, which produces the analysis and returns it to your device. We ask OpenAI not to store these requests; under its API policy it may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse and then deletes them, and it does not use them to train its models. So that a sentence someone has already asked about can be answered instantly, we store the sentence and its analysis for up to 90 days, with no account, name or other link to who sent it. We do not record which account analysed which sentence. To keep usage fair, we count how many analyses each account makes per minute and per day.

Mi'rab AI free analyses

Each person gets 3 free Mi'rab AI analyses. So that a new account does not mean new free analyses, we count them under three keys: your account ID; a one-way hash (SHA-256) of your email address, with letter case, '+' tags and Gmail dots removed; and a hash of a random ID the app creates on your device and keeps in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore. This ID is not a hardware or advertising identifier, is not shared with anyone, and is used for nothing else. This list stores your account ID, the hashed email/device keys and how many free analyses each has used; it never stores your email address itself. These counters stay after you delete your account, otherwise deleting and re-creating an account would reset them, and are deleted 12 months after they were last used. If you use Mi'rab AI after the free analyses, our server asks RevenueCat, using your account ID, whether you have an active Premium subscription.

Reporting Mi'rab AI answers

When you confirm an in-app report, we store the sentence, complete analysis, selected reason, your account ID and the submission time. Reports are private and accessible to Muhit’s administrators for content review, safety and corrections. We accept up to five reports per account per UTC day. Reports are deleted after 90 days or when you delete your account. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report. We process these reports to handle your request and protect the service, using performance of a contract and legitimate interests as applicable.

Emails

We send account emails, such as confirming your address and resetting your password, through Resend. If you write to admin@muhit.site, we use your message only to reply and keep it as long as needed to handle your request. We do not send marketing emails.

The website and cookies

muhit.site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tags, and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies, so there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner. Our host, GitHub Pages, records visitors' IP addresses in server logs for security.

No biometric data

Muhit never collects face, fingerprint or voice data. If you unlock your phone or use Sign in with Apple with Face ID or Touch ID, that check happens entirely inside your device's operating system; we never receive it.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

We process account, sync and Mi'rab AI data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We keep usage counts, the hashed free-analysis counters and security records to protect the service and its free offer from abuse (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep records where the law requires it (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).

Who processes data for us

Supabase (account database and sign-in, hosted in the EU, Ireland); OpenAI (Mi'rab AI analysis, United States); Resend (sending account emails, EU region); Hostinger (our admin@muhit.site mailbox); GitHub Pages (hosting muhit.site, which records visitors' IP addresses for security); RevenueCat (in purchase-enabled versions: managing Premium subscriptions bought in the App Store or Google Play, using your Muhit account ID when signed in or an anonymous identifier when signed out, device/app information and purchase status, United States); and Apple or Google, if you use their sign-in or buy a subscription through their stores. They may only use the data to provide their service to us. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, such as to the United States, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, synced folders, saved words, favourites, practice progress and usage counts straight away; copies in our provider's backups are overwritten within a short period. An Apple authorization token awaiting revocation is kept in a restricted retry queue until revocation succeeds, with a 30-day expiry checked by our scheduled worker. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app: see muhit.site/delete-account. Stored Mi'rab AI analyses, which are not linked to any account, are deleted after 90 days. The hashed Mi'rab AI free-analysis counters are kept after account deletion and deleted 12 months after they were last used. Store billing and RevenueCat customer records are separate from Muhit account deletion; deleting a Muhit account does not automatically delete those records or cancel billing. Contact admin@muhit.site for help with a data-deletion request involving these providers. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw any consent. Email admin@muhit.site and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY, imy.se) or the data protection authority where you live.

California and other US state privacy rights

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar US state laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling. There is therefore nothing to opt out of, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a do-not-sell-or-share request in any case. You can ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing admin@muhit.site; we will not treat you differently for using these rights. The categories we collect are listed above (identifiers such as your email address and the app's random device ID, the learning data you sync, and Premium purchase status).

Children

Accounts and Mi'rab AI are for people aged 13 or older. The offline dictionary does not collect personal data, so it can be used at any age. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects personal data, we will report it to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the GDPR requires, and tell affected users without undue delay if it is likely to put their rights at high risk, including what happened and what they can do. We also notify users and authorities where other laws, such as US state breach-notification laws, require it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Muhit's data handling changes, and show the date of the latest version at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
